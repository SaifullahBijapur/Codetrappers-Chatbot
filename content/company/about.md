CodeTrappers is an online platform for learning to code. It offers structured courses, tutorials, a community forum and help resources.

## What we offer

- Self-paced courses with lessons, exercises and projects.
- Tutorials and code examples for common languages.
- A community forum with moderated answers.
- Help pages for billing, accounts and technical issues.

## Who it is for

Beginners starting out, career changers, and working developers who want to add new skills.
