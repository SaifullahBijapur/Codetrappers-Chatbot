## I forgot my password

Choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and follow the link sent to your email. The link expires after 60 minutes.

## How do I change my email address?

Account > Profile > Email. You will need to confirm the new address.

## How do I delete my account?

Account > Privacy > Delete account. This permanently removes your progress and certificates.
