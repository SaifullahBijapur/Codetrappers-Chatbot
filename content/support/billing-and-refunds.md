# Billing and Refunds

SAMPLE CONTENT: placeholder policy, replace with your real policy.

## How do I get a refund?

You can request a refund within 14 days of purchase if you have completed less than 20% of the course. Go to Account > Billing > Request refund, or contact support with your order number.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Account > Billing > Cancel plan. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

## Which payment methods are accepted?

Major credit and debit cards.

## Where can I find my invoice?

Account > Billing > Invoices. Invoices can be downloaded as PDF.
