# 17 Free Yet Powerful Tools To Check Your WordPress Website Regularly

Yusuf Mudagal 29 Dec 2022 3 min read

Welcome to our blog at CodeTrappers! In this post, we will be discussing 17 free yet powerful tools that you can use to regularly check your WordPress website.

1.  WordPress Health Check & Troubleshooting: This plugin helps you identify and fix common issues on your WordPress site. It also provides suggestions on how to improve your site’s performance and security.
2.  Broken Link Checker: As the name suggests, this plugin helps you find and fix broken links on your WordPress site. It can also alert you to links that are no longer working, so you can take action to fix them.
3.  W3 Total Cache: This plugin helps improve the performance of your WordPress site by caching pages and posts, reducing the load on your server. It also integrates with cloud-based content delivery networks (CDN) to further improve your site’s loading speed.
4.  WordFence Security: This plugin helps secure your WordPress site by protecting against [malware](https://codetrappers.com/malware-removal-for-wordpress/), hacking attempts, and other security threats. It also includes features like a firewall and malware scanning to keep your site safe.
5.  Gravity Forms: This plugin allows you to create custom forms for your WordPress site, such as contact forms, survey forms, and more. It also includes integrations with popular email marketing tools like Mailchimp.
6.  WPForms: Similar to Gravity Forms, WPForms allows you to create custom forms for your WordPress site. It also includes features like form analytics and spam protection to help you optimize your forms.
7.  Yoast SEO: This plugin helps you optimize your WordPress site for search engines, including Google. It includes features like keyword optimization, sitemap creation, and more.
8.  Jetpack: This plugin includes a variety of tools to help you manage and optimize your WordPress site, including site analytics, security features, and [performance optimization](https://codetrappers.com/page-speed-optimization-for-wordpress/) tools.
9.  UpdraftPlus: This plugin allows you to easily back up your WordPress site, including your database, plugins, and themes. It also includes integrations with cloud-based storage services like Google Drive and Dropbox.
10.  Advanced Custom Fields: This plugin allows you to easily add custom fields to your WordPress site, making it easy to add extra content or data to your posts and pages.
11.  WP Smush: This plugin helps optimize your images for faster loading times, which can improve the overall performance of your WordPress site. It also includes features like automatic image resizing and lazy loading.
12.  Query Monitor: This plugin helps you debug and optimize your WordPress site by showing you detailed information about the queries being run on your site, as well as any errors or performance issues.
13.  Redirection: This plugin allows you to easily manage redirects on your WordPress site, which can be useful if you’re changing the URLs of your pages or posts.
14.  Cloudflare: This plugin integrates your WordPress site with Cloudflare, a CDN and security service that can help improve the performance and security of your site.
15.  Advanced Cron Manager: This plugin allows you to easily manage and optimize the cron jobs on your WordPress site, which can help improve the performance and efficiency of your site.
16.  Autoptimize: This plugin helps optimize your WordPress site by minifying and combining your CSS, JavaScript, and HTML files, which can improve your site’s loading speed.
17.  W3C Validator: This tool allows you to check your WordPress site for any HTML or CSS errors, ensuring that your site is properly coded and follows best practices.

We hope these tools will be helpful in regularly checking and optimizing your WordPress site.
