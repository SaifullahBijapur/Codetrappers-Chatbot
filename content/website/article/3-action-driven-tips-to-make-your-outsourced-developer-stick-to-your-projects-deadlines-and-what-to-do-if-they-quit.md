# 3 Action-Driven Tips To Make Your Outsourced Developer Stick To Your Project’s Deadlines (And What To Do If They Quit)

As a business owner, it’s crucial that you have a reliable and dedicated development team working on your project. However, when you outsource your development work, it can be difficult to ensure that your team sticks to the deadlines you’ve set. Here are three action-driven tips to make your outsourced developer, like CodeTrappers, stick to your project’s deadlines and what to do if they quit.

## Set clear goals and expectations from the start

It’s important to set clear goals and expectations from the beginning of the project to ensure that everyone is on the same page. This includes setting deadlines for each task and clearly outlining the scope of the project. By having a clear understanding of what is expected, your outsourced developer will be able to plan their work accordingly and meet the deadlines you’ve set.

## Communicate regularly

Effective communication is key to the success of any project. Make sure to have regular check-ins with your outsourced developer to discuss progress and address any issues that may arise. This will allow you to stay updated on the status of the project and ensure that everything is on track. Additionally, having open and honest communication will allow you to build a strong working relationship with your developer, which can lead to better results.

## Use project management tools

There are many project management tools available that can help you stay organized and on track with your project. These tools allow you to assign tasks, set deadlines, and track progress. By using these tools, you can stay informed about the status of your project and ensure that your outsourced developer is meeting their deadlines.

If your outsourced developer quits or is unable to complete the project, it’s important to have a plan in place. First, try to identify the root cause of the issue. Was it a miscommunication or misunderstanding? Was the scope of the project not clearly defined? Once you’ve identified the cause, you can work with your developer to find a solution or consider finding a new developer to take on the project.

In conclusion, effective communication, clear goals and expectations, and the use of project management tools can help ensure that your outsourced developer sticks to the deadlines you’ve set for your project. If an issue does arise, it’s important to address it quickly and find a solution to ensure that your project stays on track. By following these action-driven tips, you can build a strong and reliable development team with CodeTrappers or any other outsourced developer.
