# 3 Core Areas To Increase Your Agency Margins Without Touching Your Pricing Strategy

Yusuf Mudagal 24 Dec 2022 2 min read

As an agency, it’s important to constantly look for ways to increase your margins without sacrificing the quality of your work or alienating your clients. One way to do this is by focusing on three key areas: streamlining your processes, finding new revenue streams, and optimizing your team’s productivity.

1.  Streamlining your processes: As an agency, it’s likely that you have a number of different processes in place to manage your projects and deliver your services. By streamlining these processes, you can save time and reduce costs, which can increase your margins. For example, you might consider using project management software to help you manage your projects more efficiently, or automating certain tasks using tools like Zapier or IFTTT.
2.  Finding new revenue streams: Another way to increase your agency’s margins is by finding new sources of revenue. This might involve diversifying your services, finding new clients, or expanding into new markets. For example, if your agency primarily provides web design services, you might consider offering additional services like SEO or social media marketing to your clients.
3.  Optimizing your team’s productivity: Finally, you can increase your agency’s margins by optimizing your team’s productivity. This might involve finding ways to reduce distractions, streamlining communication, or implementing tools and processes that help your team work more efficiently. For example, you might consider using collaboration software like Slack or Trello to help your team stay organized and connected, or implementing a project management system like Asana or Basecamp to help you stay on top of your tasks.

By focusing on these three areas, CodeTrappers can increase its margins without touching its pricing strategy. By streamlining its processes, finding new revenue streams, and optimizing its team’s productivity, the agency can deliver high-quality work to its clients while still maintaining its profitability.
