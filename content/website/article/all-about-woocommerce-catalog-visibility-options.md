# All About WooCommerce Catalog Visibility Options

Welcome to our blog on "All About WooCommerce Catalog Visibility Options" by CodeTrappers!

Yusuf Mudagal 22 Dec 2022 2 min read

Welcome to our blog on “All About [WooCommerce](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/) Catalog Visibility Options” by CodeTrappers!

As an online store owner, it’s important to have control over what products are displayed to your customers. That’s where WooCommerce catalog visibility options come in. With these options, you can choose which products are visible on your website, and to whom they are visible.

There are four main catalog visibility options in WooCommerce:

1.  Public: This is the default setting, and means that all products are visible to everyone.
2.  Private: Private products are only visible to logged-in customers. This can be useful if you want to offer special deals or discounts to registered users.
3.  Hidden: Hidden products are not visible on the front-end of your website, but can still be accessed by direct link. This can be useful for creating a product catalog for wholesale customers, for example.
4.  Password protected: Similar to hidden products, password protected products are not visible on the front-end of your website. However, they can only be accessed by customers who have the correct password. This can be a good option for creating exclusive product launches or events.

It’s easy to change the visibility of your products in WooCommerce. Simply go to the product edit screen and look for the “Visibility” option in the “Product Data” section. From there, you can choose the visibility setting that best suits your needs.

In addition to these options, WooCommerce also has a feature called “Catalog Visibility by User Role.” This allows you to specify which user roles can see certain products. For example, you could set it up so that only administrators and shop managers can see certain products, while regular customers cannot.

In conclusion, WooCommerce catalog visibility options give you the flexibility to control which products are visible to your customers, and to whom they are visible. Whether you want to offer special deals to registered users, create a wholesale catalog, or launch exclusive products, these options have you covered.
