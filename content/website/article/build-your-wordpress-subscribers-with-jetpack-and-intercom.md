# Build Your WordPress Subscribers with Jetpack and Intercom

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! In today's post, we'll be discussing how you can build your WordPress subscribers list with Jetpack and Intercom.

Yusuf Mudagal 30 Dec 2022 2 min read

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! In today’s post, we’ll be discussing how you can build your WordPress subscribers list with Jetpack and Intercom.

First, let’s start with Jetpack. Jetpack is a plugin for WordPress that offers a variety of features to help you manage and grow your website. One of those features is the ability to create a newsletter subscription form for your website.

To create a subscription form with Jetpack, you’ll need to have the Jetpack plugin installed and activated on your WordPress site. Once you have it set up, you can go to the Jetpack menu in your WordPress dashboard and click on the “Forms” option. From here, you can create a new form and choose the “Newsletter” form type.

You can customize the form to your liking, including the form fields and the design. Once you’re happy with your form, you can click on the “Publish” button to make it live on your website.

Now, let’s move on to Intercom. Intercom is a customer communication platform that allows you to easily connect with your website visitors and customers. One of the features of Intercom is the ability to create a newsletter signup form.

To create a newsletter signup form with Intercom, you’ll need to have the Intercom plugin installed and activated on your WordPress site. Once it’s set up, you can go to the Intercom menu in your WordPress dashboard and click on the “Signup Form” option. From here, you can create a new form and customize it to your liking.

You can choose the fields you want to include in your form, as well as the design and placement of the form on your website. Once you’re happy with your form, you can click on the “Publish” button to make it live on your website.

Both Jetpack and Intercom offer a simple and effective way to build your WordPress subscribers list. By using these tools, you can easily grow your email list and keep your website visitors and customers informed about your latest content and updates.
