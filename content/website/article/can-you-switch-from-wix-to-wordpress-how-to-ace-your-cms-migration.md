# Can You Switch From Wix to WordPress? How to Ace Your CMS Migration

* * *

When it comes to building and managing a website, choosing the right content management system (CMS) is crucial. Wix and WordPress are two of the most popular CMS options available, and while they both have their pros and cons, you may find that WordPress is a better fit for your needs. If you’re considering making the switch from Wix to WordPress, here’s what you need to know.

Why switch from Wix to WordPress?

There are a few key reasons why you might want to switch from Wix to WordPress:

-   Customization: WordPress is known for its high level of customization, with a wide range of themes and plugins available to help you build the perfect website. Wix also offers customization options, but they tend to be more limited.
-   SEO: WordPress is generally considered to be more SEO-friendly than Wix, which can make it easier for your website to rank well in search results.
-   Scalability: As your business grows, you may find that Wix’s limitations become more apparent. WordPress, on the other hand, is highly scalable and can grow with your business.
-   Flexibility: WordPress is an open-source CMS, which means it’s constantly being updated and improved by a community of developers. This makes it a highly flexible platform that can adapt to your needs. Wix, on the other hand, is a proprietary platform, so you’re limited to the features and functionality provided by the company.

How to switch from Wix to WordPress

Switching from Wix to WordPress can seem intimidating, but it doesn’t have to be. With the right tools and guidance, you can ace your CMS [migration](https://codetrappers.com/migration-for-wordpress/) and have your new WordPress site up and running in no time. Here are the steps you’ll need to take:

## Export your content from Wix

The first step in switching from Wix to WordPress is to export your content from Wix. To do this, log into your Wix account and go to the dashboard. From there, click on the “Export & Import” tab in the left-hand menu. You’ll be given the option to export your site’s content as an XML file.

## Set up a WordPress site

Next, you’ll need to set up a WordPress site. There are a few different ways to do this, including installing WordPress on your own hosting or using a managed WordPress hosting provider. Once you’ve chosen a hosting option and set up your site, it’s time to import your content.

## Import your content into WordPress

To import your content into WordPress, you’ll need to use the WordPress Importer plugin. First, log into your WordPress site and go to the dashboard. From there, click on the “Plugins” tab in the left-hand menu and select “Add New.” Search for the WordPress Importer plugin and install it. Once it’s installed, click on the “Activate” button to turn it on.

Next, go to the “Tools” tab in the left-hand menu and select “Import.” Choose the “WordPress” option from the list of available importers and click on the “Install Now” button. Once the plugin is installed, click on the “Run Importer” button to start the import process.

On the next screen, you’ll be asked to upload the XML file that you exported from Wix. Once you’ve selected the file, click on the “Upload file and import” button. WordPress will then import your content, including posts, pages, and media.
