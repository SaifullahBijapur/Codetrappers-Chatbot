# How To (Happily) Survive Upgrading Your Website To WooCommerce 3.0 (Along With Some Of Its Common Issues)

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! In this post, we'll be discussing how to successfully upgrade your website to WooCommerce 3.0, as well as some common issues

Yusuf Mudagal 29 Dec 2022 3 min read

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! In this post, we’ll be discussing how to successfully upgrade your website to [WooCommerce](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/) 3.0, as well as some common issues that may arise during the process.

Upgrading to WooCommerce 3.0 can be a daunting task, especially if you’re not familiar with the ins and outs of the platform. However, with a little bit of preparation and knowledge, you can make the transition smoothly and happily.

Before we get started, it’s important to note that you should always create a backup of your website before attempting any major updates or changes. This will ensure that you have a fallback plan in case something goes wrong during the upgrade process.

Now, let’s dive into the steps for upgrading to WooCommerce 3.0:

1.  Update your WordPress and WooCommerce plugins: Before upgrading to WooCommerce 3.0, make sure that your WordPress and all related plugins are up to date. This will ensure that everything is running smoothly and reduce the risk of encountering issues during the upgrade.
2.  Deactivate and delete old extensions: If you have any old or unused extensions on your website, now is the time to deactivate and delete them. This will help to streamline the upgrade process and make it easier to troubleshoot any issues that may arise.
3.  Install the WooCommerce 3.0 update: Once you’ve completed the first two steps, it’s time to install the WooCommerce 3.0 update. This can be done through the WordPress dashboard by going to “Plugins” and selecting “Add New.” From there, search for “WooCommerce” and select the “Update Now” button when it appears.
4.  Test your website: After the update has been installed, it’s important to test your website to make sure everything is functioning properly. This includes checking your products, orders, and payment gateways to ensure they are all working correctly.

Now that you’ve successfully upgraded to WooCommerce 3.0, it’s time to address some of the common issues that may arise during the process.

One issue you may encounter is compatibility issues with your theme or plugins. If you find that your theme or plugins are not compatible with the new version of WooCommerce, you may need to upgrade or replace them in order to get everything running smoothly.

Another common issue is broken links or images on your website. This can happen if your website was heavily customized and the update doesn’t take these customizations into account. To fix this issue, you may need to manually update your links and images to ensure they are working properly.

Finally, you may encounter issues with your payment gateways or shipping options. If this is the case, you may need to update or reconfigure these settings to ensure that everything is working properly.

In conclusion, upgrading to WooCommerce 3.0 can be a challenging but rewarding task. By following the steps outlined above and being prepared to troubleshoot any issues that may arise, you can make the transition smoothly and happily.
