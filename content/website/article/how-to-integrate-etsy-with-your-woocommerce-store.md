# How to Integrate Etsy With Your WooCommerce Store

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! In this post, we'll be showing you how to integrate your Etsy store with your WooCommerce website using the Etsy Importer

Yusuf Mudagal 23 Dec 2022 2 min read

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! In this post, we’ll be showing you how to integrate your Etsy store with your [WooCommerce](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/) website using the Etsy Importer plugin.

First, let’s start by discussing why you might want to integrate your Etsy store with WooCommerce. By syncing your Etsy store with WooCommerce, you can expand your reach and potentially increase your sales. You’ll be able to display your Etsy products on your own website, which can give you more control over the look and feel of your online store. Plus, you’ll have access to all the features and tools that WooCommerce offers, such as customizable shipping options, coupon codes, and detailed sales reports.

Now, let’s get started with the integration process.

1.  Install the Etsy Importer plugin on your WooCommerce website. You can do this by going to the “Plugins” section in your WordPress dashboard and clicking “Add New.” Search for “Etsy Importer” and click “Install Now.” Once the plugin is installed, click “Activate.”
2.  Connect your Etsy account to the plugin. To do this, you’ll need to go to the “Etsy Importer” settings page in your WordPress dashboard. Click the “Connect to Etsy” button and follow the prompts to authorize the plugin to access your Etsy account.
3.  Select the Etsy listings you want to import. On the same settings page, you’ll see a list of all your Etsy listings. Check the boxes next to the listings you want to import and click the “Import Selected” button.
4.  Review and edit your imported products. Once the import process is complete, you’ll see a list of all your imported products in the WooCommerce Products section of your dashboard. You can review and edit these products just like any other WooCommerce product.
5.  Set up your payment and shipping options. Go to the WooCommerce Settings page and click on the “Payment” and “Shipping” tabs to set up your preferred payment and shipping options. These options will apply to all your products, including the ones you imported from Etsy.

And that’s it! You’ve successfully integrated your Etsy store with your WooCommerce website. With the Etsy Importer plugin, it’s easy to keep your products in sync across both platforms, so you can focus on running your business and making sales.
