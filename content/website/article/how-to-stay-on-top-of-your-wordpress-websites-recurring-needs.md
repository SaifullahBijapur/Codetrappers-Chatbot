# How To Stay On Top Of Your WordPress Website’s Recurring Needs

Yusuf Mudagal 28 Dec 2022 2 min read

As a website owner, it’s important to stay on top of the recurring needs of your WordPress site to ensure that it remains secure, performs well, and provides a positive user experience. Here are a few tips from CodeTrappers on how to stay on top of your WordPress website’s recurring needs:

1.  Keep your WordPress core, themes, and plugins up to date. WordPress releases updates regularly to fix security vulnerabilities and improve the platform’s performance. It’s important to keep your WordPress core, themes, and plugins up to date to ensure that your site is secure and running smoothly.
2.  Perform regular [backups](https://codetrappers.com/maintenance-for-wordpress/). In case something goes wrong with your site, it’s important to have a recent backup that you can restore. There are several plugins available that can automate the backup process for you.
3.  Monitor your site’s performance. If your site is slow to load or experiencing other performance issues, it can impact your user experience and search engine rankings. Use tools like Google PageSpeed Insights or GTMetrix to monitor your site’s performance and identify any issues that need to be addressed.
4.  Monitor your site’s security. Hackers are constantly trying to find vulnerabilities in WordPress sites, so it’s important to monitor your site’s security and take steps to prevent attacks. Use a security plugin like Wordfence to scan for security vulnerabilities and block malicious traffic.
5.  Monitor your site’s traffic and analytics. Use tools like Google Analytics to track your site’s traffic and understand how visitors are interacting with your site. This can help you identify any areas for improvement and optimize your site for better performance.

By following these tips from CodeTrappers, you can stay on top of your WordPress website’s recurring needs and keep it running smoothly.
