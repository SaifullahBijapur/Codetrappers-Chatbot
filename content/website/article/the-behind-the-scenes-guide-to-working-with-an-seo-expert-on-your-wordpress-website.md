If you’re a business owner or digital marketer looking to improve the search engine ranking of your WordPress website, you may have considered working with an SEO expert. But what exactly does an SEO expert do, and how can you get the most out of your collaboration with one? In this blog post, we’ll take a behind-the-scenes look at the process of working with an SEO expert at CodeTrappers to optimize your WordPress website.

First and foremost, it’s important to understand that SEO is an ongoing process, not a one-time fix. An SEO expert will work with you to identify areas of improvement on your website and develop a strategy to optimize those areas over time. This may involve conducting keyword research to identify the most effective terms to target, optimizing the content and structure of your website to make it more search engine friendly, and building high-quality backlinks to improve your website’s authority in the eyes of search engines.

One of the first steps in working with an SEO expert is to conduct a website audit. This involves analyzing your website’s current performance in search engines, identifying any technical issues or on-page optimization opportunities, and establishing a baseline for future progress. The audit may also include an analysis of your website’s backlink profile, to identify any potentially harmful links that may be negatively impacting your search engine ranking.

Based on the results of the audit, the SEO expert will develop a customized optimization plan for your website. This plan may include recommendations for on-page optimization, such as updating title tags and meta descriptions to include targeted keywords, optimizing images and videos for search engines, and improving the overall user experience of your website. The plan may also include off-page optimization strategies, such as building high-quality backlinks from other reputable websites.

Once the optimization plan has been developed, the SEO expert will work with you to implement the recommended changes on your website. This may involve updating the content and structure of your website, as well as making technical changes to the website’s code. The expert will also work with you to track the progress of the optimization efforts and make any necessary adjustments along the way.

It’s important to remember that SEO is a long-term process, and it can take several months to see significant improvements in search engine ranking. However, with a strategic approach and consistent effort, an SEO expert can help you achieve measurable results and drive more qualified traffic to your website.
