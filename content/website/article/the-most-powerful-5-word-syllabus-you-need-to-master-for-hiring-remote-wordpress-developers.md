# The Most Powerful 5-Word Syllabus You Need To Master For Hiring Remote WordPress Developers

Yusuf Mudagal 28 Dec 2022 Updated 7 Aug 2026 2 min read

As a business owner or manager, you know the importance of having a reliable and skilled team of developers to handle your company’s WordPress needs. But with the rise of remote work, it can be challenging to find and hire top talent from afar. That’s where CodeTrappers comes in.

CodeTrappers is a trusted brand in the world of remote [WordPress development](https://codetrappers.com/development-for-wordpress/), and we’ve put together a syllabus of the most powerful 5 words you need to master in order to effectively hire remote WordPress developers.

## Communication

Effective communication is key in any business relationship, but it becomes even more critical when working with a remote team. You need to be able to clearly convey your expectations, deadlines, and feedback to your developers, and they need to be able to communicate their progress and any issues they may be facing.

## Trust

Trust is essential when it comes to hiring remote WordPress developers. You need to trust that they will deliver quality work on time, and they need to trust that you will provide the necessary resources and support to help them succeed.

## Responsibility

Remote WordPress developers must be able to take on a high level of responsibility, as they will often be working independently without the same level of oversight as in-office employees. This means they need to be able to manage their own time, prioritize tasks, and stay on top of deadlines.

## Flexibility

Remote work requires a high degree of flexibility, as team members may be in different time zones or facing unexpected interruptions. Your developers need to be able to adapt to changing circumstances and pivot as needed to get the job done.

## Collaboration

Collaboration is crucial for any team, but it can be more challenging when team members are not in the same physical location. Your developers need to be able to work effectively with other team members and stakeholders using tools like Slack, Asana, and Zoom.
