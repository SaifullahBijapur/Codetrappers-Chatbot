# The WooCommerce Store Manager’s Framework To Gather User Feedback On Unknown Issues

Welcome to CodeTrappers' WooCommerce Store Manager's Framework for Gathering User Feedback on Unknown Issues!

Yusuf Mudagal 26 Dec 2022 2 min read

Welcome to CodeTrappers’ [WooCommerce Store](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/) Manager’s Framework for Gathering User Feedback on Unknown Issues!

As a store manager, it can be challenging to identify and resolve issues that your customers are experiencing on your WooCommerce store. These issues may not always be apparent to you, as they may only affect a small number of users or they may not be reported to you directly.

To help you gather feedback and identify these unknown issues, we have developed a framework that you can use to actively seek out and gather user feedback. By implementing this framework, you can proactively address issues and improve the overall customer experience on your store.

Here are the steps in our framework:

1.  Set up a system for collecting customer feedback: There are a few different ways you can do this, such as setting up a dedicated email address or using a feedback form on your website.
2.  Monitor social media and review sites: Keep an eye on social media platforms and review sites like Trustpilot to see if customers are mentioning any issues with your store. This can be an effective way to identify problems that may not have been reported directly to you.
3.  Encourage customers to leave reviews: Encourage your customers to leave reviews on your store, both positive and negative. This will give you valuable insight into what is working well and what needs improvement.
4.  Analyze customer feedback: Once you have gathered feedback from your customers, take the time to analyze it and look for trends or common themes. This will help you identify any issues that may be impacting a large number of customers.
5.  Act on customer feedback: Once you have identified an issue, it’s important to take action to resolve it. This could involve making changes to your website, improving your customer service, or implementing new policies.

By following these steps, you can effectively gather user feedback and identify unknown issues on your WooCommerce store. This will help you improve the customer experience and keep your customers coming back for more.
