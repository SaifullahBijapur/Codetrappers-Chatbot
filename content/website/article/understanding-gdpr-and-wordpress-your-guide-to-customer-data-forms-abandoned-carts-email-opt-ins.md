As a website owner, it’s important to understand the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and how it applies to your WordPress site. GDPR is a set of rules that aims to protect the personal data of EU citizens and give them more control over how their data is collected and used. If you collect, store, or process the personal data of EU citizens, you need to be compliant with GDPR.

One area where GDPR applies to WordPress sites is in the collection and use of customer data. This includes information like names, email addresses, and addresses, which may be collected through forms, abandoned cart recovery emails, or email opt-ins.

To be compliant with GDPR, you need to be transparent about the data you collect and how it will be used. This means that you need to include a privacy policy on your website and ensure that it’s easy for visitors to find. You should also make sure that any forms or email opt-ins include clear and concise information about what data is being collected and how it will be used.

Abandoned cart recovery emails are a common feature of e-commerce websites, but under GDPR, you need to be careful about how you use this data. You can only send abandoned cart recovery emails to customers who have given their explicit consent to receive them. This means that you need to include a clear opt-in for these emails when customers are making a purchase or signing up for your newsletter.
