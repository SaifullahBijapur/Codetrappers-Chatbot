# What Will Gutenberg Mean For My WooCommerce Store?

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! Today, we're going to be discussing an exciting new development in the world of WooCommerce: the Gutenberg editor.

Yusuf Mudagal 24 Dec 2022 2 min read

Welcome to the CodeTrappers blog! Today, we’re going to be discussing an exciting new development in the world of [WooCommerce](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/): the Gutenberg editor.

For those who are unfamiliar, Gutenberg is a new content editor for WordPress that was developed by the WordPress team. It replaces the classic editor, which many users have grown accustomed to over the years. Gutenberg is designed to be more user-friendly and intuitive, making it easier for users to create and edit content on their WordPress sites.

So, what does this mean for your WooCommerce store? Well, there are a few key things to consider.

First and foremost, Gutenberg will make it easier for you to create and edit product pages on your WooCommerce store. With the classic editor, adding and formatting content could be a bit of a hassle, but Gutenberg simplifies the process by providing a more intuitive interface. This means that you’ll be able to create and update your product pages more quickly and easily, which is great news for anyone who manages a busy online store.

Another important benefit of Gutenberg is that it allows you to create more visually appealing product pages. With the classic editor, it could be difficult to add custom formatting or layout elements to your pages. However, Gutenberg makes it easy to add things like columns, images, and other visual elements to your pages, which can help to make your products stand out. This can be especially useful for anyone who is trying to sell visually appealing products, like clothing or home decor.

Finally, Gutenberg also makes it easier for you to create custom blocks for your product pages. With the classic editor, you were limited to using pre-designed blocks for your pages. However, Gutenberg allows you to create your own custom blocks, which can be a great way to add unique elements to your pages and make them stand out from the competition.

Overall, the Gutenberg editor is a great addition to WooCommerce that will make it easier for you to create and edit product pages on your store. If you haven’t already made the switch, we highly recommend giving it a try – we think you’ll love it!
