A small task in [WordPress development](https://codetrappers.com/development-for-wordpress/) refers to a relatively simple and quick task that can be completed within a short period of time. These tasks may include minor customizations, updates, or fixes to a WordPress website.

Some examples of small tasks in WordPress development include:

1.  Updating a plugin or theme: If you need to update a plugin or theme on your WordPress site, this is considered a small task. The cost will depend on the complexity of the update and the hourly rate of the developer you are working with.
2.  Adding a new form or contact form: If you need to add a new form or contact form to your WordPress site, this is also considered a small task. The cost will depend on the complexity of the form and the hourly rate of the developer you are working with.
3.  Customizing the appearance of your site: If you want to make small changes to the appearance of your site, such as changing the color scheme or adding a new header image, this is considered a small task.
