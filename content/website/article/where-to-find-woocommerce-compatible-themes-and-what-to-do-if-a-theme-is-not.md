# Where To Find WooCommerce Compatible Themes (And What To Do If A Theme Is Not)

Are you looking for a new theme for your WooCommerce store, but not sure where to find one that's compatible? Look no further! CodeTrappers has you covered.

Yusuf Mudagal 28 Dec 2022 2 min read

Are you looking for a new theme for your [WooCommerce store](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/), but not sure where to find one that’s compatible? Look no further! CodeTrappers has you covered.

First, let’s start with where to find WooCommerce compatible themes. One option is to visit the official WooCommerce theme library, which features a collection of themes that have been tested and certified to work seamlessly with the WooCommerce plugin. These themes come from a variety of different developers, including CodeTrappers, so it’s a great place to start your search.

Another option is to search for WooCommerce compatible themes on popular theme marketplaces such as ThemeForest or Creative Market. These marketplaces feature a wide range of themes from different developers, including CodeTrappers, so you’ll have plenty of options to choose from. Just make sure to double-check that the theme you’re considering is compatible with WooCommerce before making a purchase.

If you’re unable to find a theme that meets your needs in the above mentioned places, don’t worry! There are still a few options available to you.

One option is to contact the theme developer directly and ask if they offer support for WooCommerce. Many developers are willing to add WooCommerce compatibility to their themes upon request, so it’s worth reaching out and asking.

Another option is to use a theme that is not specifically designed for WooCommerce, but is still compatible. This may require some extra setup and configuration on your part, but it can be a great way to find a theme that perfectly matches the look and feel you’re going for.

If you do decide to go this route, be sure to carefully read through the theme’s documentation and reach out to the developer for support if you have any questions or run into any issues.

In conclusion, there are plenty of options available for finding WooCommerce compatible themes. Whether you choose to go with a theme from the official WooCommerce theme library, a popular theme marketplace, or a theme that requires a bit of extra setup, CodeTrappers is here to help you every step of the way. So, if you need any assistance in finding the perfect theme for your WooCommerce store, don’t hesitate to reach out to us!
