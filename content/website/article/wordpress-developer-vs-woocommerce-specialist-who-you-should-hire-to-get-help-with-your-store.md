# WordPress Developer vs WooCommerce Specialist: Who You Should Hire To Get Help With Your Store

Yusuf Mudagal 27 Dec 2022 3 min read

Are you looking to hire someone to help with your online store? As a business owner, it’s important to understand the different roles and expertise that can be brought to the table when it comes to developing and maintaining an e-commerce site. In this blog, we’ll explore the difference between a WordPress developer and a [WooCommerce](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/) specialist, and help you decide which type of professional is right for your needs.

First, let’s define each role:

A WordPress developer is a professional who is skilled in developing and customizing websites using the WordPress content management system (CMS). This includes tasks such as theme customization, [plugin development](https://codetrappers.com/custom-plugin-development/), and core updates. WordPress developers generally have a broad range of skills, including front-end development, database management, and server-side coding.

On the other hand, a WooCommerce specialist is a professional who is specifically focused on the WooCommerce plugin for WordPress. WooCommerce is a popular e-commerce plugin that allows users to sell products and services directly through their WordPress site. A WooCommerce specialist is an expert in all things related to the plugin, including setting up and configuring the plugin, customizing the store, and troubleshooting any issues that may arise.

So, who should you hire to help with your online store? It really depends on your specific needs. If you’re looking for someone to help with general [WordPress development](https://codetrappers.com/development-for-wordpress/) tasks, such as theme customization or plugin development, a WordPress developer may be the way to go. However, if you’re specifically looking for someone to help with your WooCommerce store, a WooCommerce specialist may be the better choice.
