# WordPress pricing debunked: How much does a WordPress website cost? How much for an e-commerce based on WordPress?

Yusuf Mudagal 30 Dec 2022 Updated 7 Aug 2026 3 min read

WordPress Pricing Debunked: How Much Does a WordPress Website Cost?

If you’re considering building a website with WordPress, you may have heard that it’s a budget-friendly option. But when it comes to pricing, it can be difficult to get a clear answer on exactly how much a WordPress website will cost. In this blog post, we’ll debunk the mystery of WordPress pricing and give you a better understanding of what you can expect to pay for a WordPress website.

## How Much Does a Basic WordPress Website Cost?

A basic WordPress website typically costs around $3,000 to $5,000. This price includes the cost of a domain name, hosting, and the design and development of the website.

Keep in mind that this price is just a rough estimate and can vary depending on the specific needs and requirements of your website. For example, if you need a lot of custom functionality or have a complex design, your website may cost more. On the other hand, if you have a simple design and only need a few basic features, your website may cost less.

## How Much Does an e-Commerce WordPress Website Cost?

An e-commerce WordPress website typically costs around $5,000 to $10,000. This price includes the cost of a domain name, hosting, design and development of the website, and the integration of an e-commerce platform.

Again, this price is just a rough estimate and can vary depending on the specific needs and requirements of your website. If you have a large product catalog or need a lot of custom functionality, your website may cost more. On the other hand, if you have a small product catalog and only need a few basic features, your website may cost less.

## What About Ongoing Costs for a WordPress Website?

In addition to the initial cost of building a WordPress website, there are also ongoing costs to consider. These costs can include:

-   Hosting: You’ll need to pay for hosting in order to keep your website online. Hosting costs can range from $10 to $30 per month, depending on the hosting provider and the specific plan you choose.
-   Maintenance: You’ll need to regularly update your WordPress website to ensure it stays secure and functions properly. This can include tasks such as plugin updates, theme updates, and general [website maintenance](https://codetrappers.com/maintenance-for-wordpress/). Maintenance costs can range from $100 to $500 per month, depending on the specific needs of your website.
-   Marketing: If you want to drive traffic to your website, you’ll need to invest in marketing efforts such as SEO, social media marketing, and advertising. Marketing costs can vary widely, depending on the specific tactics you use and the budget you have available.
-   E-commerce costs: If you have an e-commerce website, you’ll also need to consider the cost of processing transactions, including any fees charged by your payment gateway and any additional e-commerce software or services you use.
