## The Challenge

A growing **B2B eCommerce enterprise** required a robust **marketplace platform** that could handle:

-   Bulk order management with tiered pricing and supplier-based approvals.
-   Complex shipping calculations based on container size, weight, and freight charges.
-   Multi-vendor setup with custom pricing models for different buyers.
-   Automated order workflows to reduce manual processes.
-   A modern, intuitive UI/UX to enhance the B2B purchasing experience.

The client needed a **fully customized B2B [WooCommerce](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/) marketplace** that could support **large-scale wholesale transactions, freight-based shipping, and seamless vendor-buyer interactions**.

## The Solution

We designed and built a **feature-rich B2B marketplace** with advanced capabilities:

##### Custom Bulk Order Management System

-   Enabled bulk order pricing tiers with dynamic discounts.
-   Developed vendor-specific product availability & MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) rules.
-   Implemented RFQ (Request for Quote) system for negotiation-based ordering.

##### Advanced Freight & Shipping Calculations

-   Created container-based freight shipping calculators based on weight, volume, and location.
-   Integrated multi-carrier shipping options with automated rate calculations.
-   Enabled split-shipping for large bulk orders, reducing logistics costs.

##### Seamless Multi-Vendor & Wholesale Functionality

-   Developed vendor dashboards for order tracking & bulk invoicing.
-   Integrated customized tax rules & pricing per vendor category.
-   Centralized Enabled direct vendor-buyer communication for personalized deals. partner integration via Shiprocket for wider reach.

##### Modern UI/UX for a Streamlined Shopping Experience

-   Designed an intuitive B2B-focused interface optimized for large transactions.
-   Created a responsive, mobile-friendly design for seamless order placements.
-   Built custom dashboards for buyers & vendors to track orders, shipments & payments.

##### Automated Workflows for Efficient Order Processing

-   Implemented order approval, invoicing & fulfillment automation.
-   Developed AI-driven demand forecasting & inventory tracking.
-   Integrated ERP & accounting software for real-time financial management.
