# Modernizing Online Furniture Shopping with a Custom Build on WooCommerce

A furniture retailer was losing people at the delivery step. We rebuilt pricing, shipping and the follow-up that happens when a cart is abandoned.

Industry

Luxury & Modern Furniture eCommerce

2 Months

## The Challenge

A high-end **modern furniture brand** wanted to elevate its online shopping experience but faced several roadblocks:

-   Generic discounting models that didn’t account for targeted promotions.
-   No marketing automation, leading to lost customer engagement.
-   Complex shipping needs, requiring custom pricing based on weight, location, and furniture type.
-   Lack of visual shopping experiences, making it hard for customers to imagine pieces in a real-world setting.
-   No personalized marketing pop-ups, leading to missed conversion opportunities.

The client needed a **fully customized [WooCommerce store](https://codetrappers.com/wordpress-for-ecommerce/)** with advanced **discounting, marketing automation, interactive shopping experiences, and optimized shipping calculations** to boost engagement and conversions.

## The Solution

We built a **high-performance WooCommerce furniture store** with cutting-edge features:

##### Custom Discounting Model

-   Developed dynamic discount logic based on product type, cart value, and customer behavior.
-   Enabled automatic tiered pricing and personalized offers for repeat customers.

##### Custom Shipping Methods & Cost Calculations

-   Built custom shipping rules for large furniture vs. smaller items.
-   Integrated distance-based shipping pricing for local & nationwide delivery.
-   Enabled split-shipping for multi-item orders to optimize logistics.

##### Smart Marketing Popups & Date-Based Offers

-   Designed targeted popups based on user behavior (new visitors, returning customers, exit intent).
-   Integrated limited-time and holiday-specific offers based on calendar events.

##### Shop by Looks – Interactive Room-Based Shopping

-   Developed an immersive “Shop by Room” experience, allowing customers to view fully designed spaces and purchase individual elements.
-   Integrated AI-powered product recommendations based on room aesthetics and customer preferences.

##### Seamless User Experience & Checkout Optimization

-   Created a modern, UX-optimized interface for a premium shopping feel.
-   Enabled one-click checkout & saved payment options for faster purchases.

## The Results

3x Increase in Conversions, 50% Faster Checkout, Fully Automated Marketing & Shipping Workflows

##### 3x Increase in Conversions

Personalized discounts & AI-driven promotions boosted sales.

##### 50% Faster Checkout Process

Streamlined cart & payment workflows improved customer retention.

##### Optimized Shipping Costs & Faster Deliveries

Custom shipping calculations reduced logistics delays.

##### Higher Engagement with Targeted Offers

Smart marketing popups & segmented campaigns improved customer re-engagement.

##### Enhanced Shopping Experience

“Shop by Looks” feature increased order value by showcasing complete room designs.

## Conclusion

By building a **visually immersive, fully automated WooCommerce store**, we transformed the furniture brand’s **online presence into an interactive, conversion-optimized shopping experience**. The **custom discount logic, advanced marketing workflows, and intuitive room-based shopping** helped **streamline sales, improve engagement, and boost revenue**.
