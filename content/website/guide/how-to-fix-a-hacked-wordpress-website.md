# How to Fix a Hacked WordPress Website?

3 Mar 2025 1 min read

If your WordPress site has been [hacked](https://codetrappers.com/malware-removal-for-wordpress/), don’t panic! Follow these steps:

1️⃣ **Take Your Website Offline** – Prevent further damage by disabling access.
2️⃣ **Scan for Malware** – Use security plugins like Wordfence or Sucuri.
3️⃣ **Remove Malicious Code** – Delete infected files and scripts.
4️⃣ **Update Everything** – Ensure WordPress, themes, and plugins are up to date.
5️⃣ **Secure Your Website** – Implement firewalls, limit login attempts, and enable 2FA.
