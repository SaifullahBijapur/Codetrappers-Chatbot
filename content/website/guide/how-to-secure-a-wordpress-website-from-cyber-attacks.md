# How to Secure a WordPress Website from Cyber Attacks

3 Mar 2025 1 min read

WordPress websites are a common target for hackers. To protect your site:

🔹 **Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** – Prevent unauthorized logins.
🔹 **Use a Security Plugin** – Install Wordfence or Sucuri for firewalls.
🔹 **Limit Login Attempts** – Block brute-force attacks.
🔹 **Keep WordPress & Plugins Updated** – Stay protected from vulnerabilities.
🔹 **Enable Automatic [Backups](https://codetrappers.com/maintenance-for-wordpress/)** – Recover quickly if your site is compromised.
