**Use a Lightweight Theme** – Avoid bulky themes with **excess scripts**.
**Enable Caching** – Use **WP Rocket, LiteSpeed Cache, or Cloudflare**.
**Optimize Images** – Compress images with **WebP format**.
**Reduce Plugin Bloat** – Deactivate **unnecessary plugins**.
**Use a CDN** – Distribute content across **global edge servers**.
