# What are the best WordPress SEO plugins?

Here are the top 5 WordPress SEO plugins:

3 Mar 2025 1 min read

Here are the **top 5 WordPress SEO plugins**:

1.  **Yoast SEO** – Best for on-page SEO & meta optimization.
2.  **Rank Math** – AI-powered suggestions & schema markup.
3.  **All in One SEO** – Simple interface with advanced settings.
4.  **SEOPress** – Privacy-focused SEO solution.
5.  **The SEO Framework** – Lightweight and automation-friendly.

Each plugin offers unique benefits, but **Yoast SEO and Rank Math** are the most popular choices for improving search rankings.
