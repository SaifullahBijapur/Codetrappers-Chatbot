# Why Choose Headless WordPress?

Blazing-Fast Performance – Pages load instantly using static site generation (SSG) and server-side rendering (SSR). Omnichannel Content Delivery – Publish

3 Mar 2025 1 min read

1.  **Blazing-Fast Performance** – Pages load instantly using **static site generation (SSG) and server-side rendering (SSR)**.
2.  **Omnichannel Content Delivery** – Publish content across **websites, apps, IoT, and digital platforms** from a single CMS.
3.  **Unmatched Security** – Reduces **WordPress vulnerabilities** by removing the front-end from direct access.
4.  **Scalability Without Limits** – Easily handle **high-traffic websites** without performance issues.
5.  **Full Creative Freedom** – Design using **React, Vue.js, Next.js, or any modern front-end framework**.
