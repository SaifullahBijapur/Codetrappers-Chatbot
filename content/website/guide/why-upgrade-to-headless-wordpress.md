# Why Upgrade to Headless WordPress?

3 Mar 2025 1 min read

Traditional WordPress is great, but **[headless WordPress](https://codetrappers.com/development-for-headless-wordpress/)** offers:

**Blazing Fast Load Times** – Front-end powered by React, Vue, or Next.js.
**Better Security** – API-driven structure reduces attack risks.
**Multi-Platform Content Delivery** – Serve content to websites, apps, IoT, and more.
**Custom UI & Design Freedom** – Build without WordPress theme limitations.
