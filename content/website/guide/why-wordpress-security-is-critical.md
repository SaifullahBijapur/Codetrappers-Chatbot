# Why WordPress Security is Critical?

Protects Sensitive Data – Prevents data breaches and customer leaks. Prevents Downtime – A hacked site leads to loss of business & reputation. Secures

3 Mar 2025 1 min read

-   **Protects Sensitive Data** – Prevents data breaches and customer leaks.
-   **Prevents Downtime** – A [hacked](https://codetrappers.com/malware-removal-for-wordpress/) site leads to **loss of business & reputation**.
-   **Secures Payments & Transactions** – Essential for **eCommerce stores**.
-   **Improves Trust & SEO** – Secure websites get **better rankings & user confidence**.
-   **Prevents Bot Attacks** – Reduces spam and brute-force login attempts.
