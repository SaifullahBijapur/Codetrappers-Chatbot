Our story

## How CodeTrappers started

### Why it started

We started in Bangalore in 2016. Plenty of firms could build a website; far fewer would answer the phone six months later, or tell a client the honest thing when a project was going wrong. That gap is the whole reason the company exists.

### Six people to fifteen

Yusuf Mudagal started it with five other engineers. We have grown to fifteen in ten years, which is deliberate: hiring faster than we can train people is how agencies end up putting juniors on work the client thought they were buying seniors for.

### A name that captures the essence

A defining moment took place late one evening in 2016. As the founders walked through the bustling streets of Bangalore brainstorming a name, they wanted something that symbolised problem-solving and innovation. That's when inspiration struck, **CodeTrappers**, a name that captures the idea of catching ideas and turning them into powerful code.

### Commitment to excellence

From humble beginnings, CodeTrappers has built a reputation for developing scalable, high-performance websites while delivering exceptional customer experiences. Today it continues to expand its reach, earning the trust of clients across industries and geographies.

### Staying true to core values

At its core, CodeTrappers remains dedicated to fostering a culture of learning, collaboration and problem-solving. With a strong focus on both people and technology, the company is committed to shaping the future of digital solutions while staying true to its people-first philosophy.

## What we bring to every project

### Builds, migrations and rescues

We do the building, the moving and the fixing. Most clients arrive with a site somebody else made, not a blank page.

### Built to survive updates

Standards-compliant code with tests, so the next core release is a non-event rather than a support ticket.

### Fixed scope, fixed price

Agreed in writing before work starts, including what is excluded. If we get the estimate wrong, that is our problem, not yours.

### We say when it is not us

Sometimes the honest answer is a template, a page builder, or another firm entirely. We would rather say so than take the project.

### Proven track record

102 client sites live and maintained, and 300+ migrations run without data loss. The showcase lists them.

### The same people throughout

No handover to a delivery team once you sign. The engineer who scoped the work is the engineer who does it.
