    Much of what looks like AI work is a scheduled query nobody has time to run. That part is cheaper, more reliable and faster to build without a model involved.

## What we usually end up telling people

Three findings come up often enough that you may as well know them before you commission anything.

### Start read-only

An agent that only reads and reports is worth more in month one than one with write access that nobody trusts enough to leave running.

### The bottleneck is rarely the model

It is usually access: data spread across systems that do not talk, with no interface an agent could safely use.

### Some of this is worth waiting for

Standardised WordPress abilities for creating and updating are targeted at the 7.2 cycle. If your use case sits exactly there, we will tell you to wait rather than sell you a version that gets replaced.
