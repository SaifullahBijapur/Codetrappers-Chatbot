## Inside AI Search and Knowledge Assistants

[Get in touch](https://codetrappers.com/contact/)

1.  ### Index what you actually have

    Pages, posts, PDFs, help articles, past tickets, product records. The indexing step is where most of the quality comes from, and it is the step most implementations rush.

2.  ### Retrieve before you generate

    The model only ever answers from passages pulled out of your own material. That is what stops it drawing on half-remembered training data and presenting it as your policy.

3.  ### Show the working

    Every answer carries the pages it came from. A reader who does not trust it can check in one click, which is the difference between a tool people use and a demo they abandon.

4.  ### Refuse rather than guess

    The failure that damages trust is not a wrong answer, it is a confident one. Ours are built to say when the material does not cover the question, and to hand over to a person when that is the honest response.

5.  ### Measure it with a real test set

    A set of questions with known good answers, run on every change. Without one, tuning retrieval is guesswork and every improvement is anecdotal.

## What we build

### Assistants for your public website

Answering from your own pages, in your own voice, with a handoff to a human at the point where a sale or a complaint starts.

### Internal knowledge base assistants

For the questions your team asks each other every week. Onboarding, process, policy, what happened on that account last year.

### Enterprise search across systems

One question, answered from several places at once: the intranet, the ticket history, the document store, the product catalogue.

### Search over product and catalogue data

Natural language against attributes, stock, compatibility and specification, for catalogues too large to browse.

### Evaluation and tuning

Existing assistant that answers badly? Often the cause is the index rather than the model, and that is measurable and fixable.

### Retrieval built to be replaced in part

WordPress core embeddings are being built inside the AI plugin and are expected after 7.1. We separate indexing from scoring so that when they land you replace one half and keep the rest.

### Retrieval quality is a content problem first

Our portfolio was invisible to our own search because the words visitors use, "portfolio" and "case study", appeared nowhere in the indexed text. Adding that vocabulary fixed what no amount of tuning would have.

### The dangerous answer is the confident one

An article on your site quoting somebody else's prices will be retrieved and presented as yours unless the system is told, explicitly, that it must not do that.
