The service

## What we change, and why it matters to a model

Most of this is ordinary technical SEO done carefully. The difference is who the reader is.

### Crawler access

Cloudflare and security plugins block GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot by default on many sites. We open what you want open, keep out what you do not, and log both.

### Entity clarity

Your company, your people and your services described once, consistently, with the same names and identifiers everywhere, so the model builds one entity rather than three.

### Answer-first content

The pages you already have, edited so the answer comes before the preamble. We rewrite structure, not your voice.

### FAQ and how-to markup

Real questions with real answers, marked up so they can be lifted straight into a response with attribution back to you.

### Freshness signals

Visible dates, changelogs and a publishing rhythm. Models weight recency, and a site that looks abandoned gets skipped.

### Brand mentions off-site

Directories, profiles and citations that agree with your own site. We fix the inconsistencies that make a model hedge.

## What this is not

### Not a ranking guarantee

Nobody controls what a model cites. We control whether your site is eligible, legible and consistent, then measure the result honestly.

### Not a replacement for SEO

Google still sends most of the traffic. This work strengthens ordinary search too, because both reward the same clarity.

Platforms change their crawlers and their citation behaviour often. The audit is a fixed price; keeping up is part of a retainer if you want it.
