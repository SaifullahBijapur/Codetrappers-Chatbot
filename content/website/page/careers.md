## Four things you can verify before applying

### Contributing is part of the job

We pledge ten hours a week to WordPress through Five for the Future. That time is worked, not donated out of your evenings.

[See the pledge](https://codetrappers.com/contributions/)

### You will write real code

Custom post types, meta, integrations, reviewed and versioned. Not an afternoon of dragging widgets around a page builder.

### You talk to the client

We put the engineer doing the work on the call. That is the promise we make to clients, which means it is how we work internally too.

### The community meets here

We host Bengaluru WordPress meetups at our office. You will meet the wider WordPress world without leaving the building.
