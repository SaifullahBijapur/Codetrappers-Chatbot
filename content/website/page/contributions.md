## Four things anyone can install

Reviewed by the WordPress.org plugin and theme teams, then published under the GPL. Small numbers, but real ones: every figure below comes from the WordPress.org API.

[Theme

### VYROLA

A block theme reviewed and accepted into the WordPress.org theme directory, which is a stricter bar than most people expect.

-   **30+**active installs
-   **324**downloads
-   **v1.0.1**requires WP 6.7](https://wordpress.org/themes/vyrola/)[Plugin

### Admin Toolbar Visibility Control

Hides the front-end admin toolbar per role, for stores and member sites where customers should never see it but administrators still need it.

-   **10+**active installs
-   **v2.0.4**tested to WP 7.0](https://wordpress.org/plugins/disable-admin-tool-bar/)[Plugin

### CodeTrappers Supplier Sync

Imports supplier catalogues into WooCommerce by SKU, including variations, stock and images. Written for a client, then released rather than kept private.

-   **v1.0.0**tested to WP 7.0
-   **GPL**source published](https://wordpress.org/plugins/codetrappers-supplier-sync-fashion-biz-legend-life/)[Contributed to

### Reseller Intent

An add-on for GoDaddy's Reseller Store plugin that records what people actually search for in the domain box. Maintained by Kamran Abdul Aziz; we contribute to it.

-   **v1.0.6**tested to WP 7.0](https://wordpress.org/plugins/reseller-intent/)
