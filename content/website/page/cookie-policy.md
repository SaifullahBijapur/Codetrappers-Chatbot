# Cookie Policy

Every cookie this site sets, listed by name, with what it does and how long it lasts. Analytics and advertising are blocked until you accept them.

### Cookie Policy

## What we store on your device, and how to change it

**Last updated:** 10 September 2026

This page lists every cookie and every piece of browser storage this site uses, by name. If you find something on your device from codetrappers.com that is not on this list, please [tell us](https://codetrappers.com/contact/) and we will fix the page or remove the cookie.

#### 1\. Nothing is set until you choose

Analytics and advertising cookies are blocked until you accept them. When you first arrive, a banner asks what you allow. Until you answer, our Google tag runs in a restricted mode that reports nothing identifying and writes nothing to your device.

If you accept and later change your mind, you can withdraw consent at any time using the button at the bottom of this page. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent, and refusing costs you nothing: the site works identically either way.

#### 5\. Third parties that can set their own cookies

One feature on our site links out to other companies: the **Ask AI about CodeTrappers** links in our footer, which open ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok or Perplexity. Following one takes you to that company’s site, where their policy applies rather than ours. Nothing is sent to them unless you click.

We do not use Meta, LinkedIn or TikTok pixels, and there is no Google Tag Manager container on this site.

#### 6\. Where your data goes

Analytics and advertising data is processed by Google, which may transfer it outside the country you are in. Google’s own [cookie policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies) covers what they do with it. Our [Privacy Policy](https://codetrappers.com/privacy-policy/) covers everything else we hold, including enquiries you send us.

#### 7\. Turning cookies off in your browser

Your browser can block or delete cookies independently of the choice you make here. The settings live under Privacy in Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Blocking everything will not break this site, though it will sign you out of most others.

#### 8\. Changes to this policy

If we add a tool that sets a cookie, this page gets updated and the banner asks again rather than assuming your previous answer still applies. The date at the top tells you when it last changed.
