What we stand for

## 3 beliefs that guide our studio

Yusuf Mudagal Founder, CodeTrappers

1.  ### We believe the launch is the easy part.

    Anyone can ship a website. Keeping one fast, patched and online for years is the part that decides whether building it was worth the money.

    So maintenance is not an upsell here, it is the work. 98% of our clients stay with us, and when something breaks we have triaged it within the hour.

2.  ### We believe WordPress belongs in serious infrastructure.

    WordPress runs a large share of the web and is still described as a toy, usually by people who have never had to scale one.

    We build on it the way any production system gets built: version control, tests, staging, headless where it earns its place. Global brands run on it because it was engineered to.

3.  ### We believe you should give back to what you build on.

    Our entire business sits on software somebody else maintains and then gives away for nothing.

    We contribute back through Five for the Future, to core, to plugins and to documentation. It keeps the thing we depend on healthy, and it keeps us honest about how it actually works.
