## How we stay invisible

Every white-label page in this industry says "NDA protected" and stops there. These are the specifics, and they go in the agreement.

-   A mutual NDA signed before we see anything, not after the first invoice
-   We never contact your client, on any channel, for any reason
-   No CodeTrappers credit in the footer, the source, the readme or the commit history
-   Reports and update summaries come out under your logo, ready to forward
-   We join your calls under your domain if you want us there at all
-   Nothing we build for you appears in our showcase or case studies without your written say-so
-   Credentials stay in your password manager, scoped per site, revoked when the job closes
-   Code is delivered to your repository, so the work is yours if we ever part ways

Rates depend on the stack and the volume, so they are quoted rather than listed. The model is your choice and you can change it.
