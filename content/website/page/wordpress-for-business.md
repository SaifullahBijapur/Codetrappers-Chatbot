## The same four problems, over and over

### It loads fine on your laptop

And takes eight seconds on a phone on mobile data, which is how most of your traffic arrives. Page speed is a sales problem before it is a technical one.

### Nobody can edit it

The person who built it left, the page builder licence lapsed, and now a copy change needs a developer. We hand back a site your team can actually run.

### Updates are somebody else’s job

Until a plugin goes unpatched for a year and the site gets hit. Ownership of updates, backups and monitoring is the whole point of a retainer.

### The contact form stopped sending

Quietly, months ago, and no one noticed because nothing errors. We test the paths that make you money, not just the ones that render.
