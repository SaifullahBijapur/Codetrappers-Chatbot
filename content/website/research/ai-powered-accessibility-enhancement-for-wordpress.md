# AI-Powered Accessibility Enhancement for WordPress

Research area

Web Accessibility & AI Automation

Develop an AI-driven accessibility engine that automatically enhances ADA & WCAG compliance for WordPress websites, improving inclusivity for users with disabilities.

12 Months

## The Problem Statement

Web accessibility is crucial for ensuring that all users, including those with disabilities, can navigate and interact with websites. However, most **WordPress sites** face accessibility issues due to:

-   Non-compliant design elements, such as low contrast and missing alt text.
-   Lack of screen reader support, making websites difficult for visually impaired users.
-   Unoptimized navigation, causing challenges for users with motor disabilities.
-   No automated accessibility checks, requiring expensive manual audits.

Existing accessibility plugins provide **basic features** but lack **AI-driven automation** to detect and fix accessibility issues **in real-time**.

At **CodeTrappers**, we aimed to **revolutionize WordPress accessibility** by developing an **AI-powered engine** that **automatically scans, corrects, and optimizes websites** for better inclusivity.

## The Solution: AI-Driven Web Accessibility Engine

Our **AI-powered WordPress accessibility tool** automates **WCAG & ADA compliance** by dynamically **detecting and resolving issues**.

##### AI-Based Real-Time Accessibility Scanning

-   Scans WordPress pages for contrast issues, missing alt text, and improper heading structures.
-   Uses machine learning to identify and suggest fixes for navigation and readability issues.

##### Automated Alt Text & Media Optimization

-   AI generates missing alt texts for images based on content context.
-   Optimizes video captions and transcriptions for multimedia content.

##### Dynamic Content Adjustments for Visual Impairment

-   Auto-adjusts text sizes, contrast, and color schemes for better visibility.
-   Provides keyboard navigation enhancements for users unable to use a mouse.

##### Screen Reader & Voice Navigation Support

-   Enhances compatibility with screen readers like JAWS and NVDA.
-   Introduces voice-command navigation for hands-free browsing.

##### Automated Compliance Reporting & Fixes

-   AI monitors compliance levels and provides detailed reports.
-   Generates one-click fixes for common accessibility violations.
