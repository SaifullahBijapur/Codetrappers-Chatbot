## The challenge

The client needed a central community portal and an internal employee workspace, but their existing setup had fragmented communication and no unified workflow hub.

## Our approach

We planned and built a structured community portal with role-based internal sections, collaboration-friendly information architecture, and custom modules aligned to employee operations.
Business Impact: Teams now collaborate through one reliable platform, internal communication is faster, and daily operational coordination is more consistent across departments.
