## The challenge

The client came to us with weak conversion performance: product pages were heavy, checkout had friction, and mobile users were dropping before payment.

## Our approach

We optimized templates and media delivery, simplified the product-to-checkout flow, improved trust cues, and implemented conversion-focused UX changes across key buying pages.
Business Impact: The store now loads faster, checkout completion is smoother, and the client is seeing stronger conversion consistency with higher-quality purchase traffic.
