## The challenge

The client needed better admissions outcomes because prospective students were dropping off due to unclear program details and poor mobile journeys.

## Our approach

We restructured course information, streamlined enquiry forms, improved mobile readability, and optimized page speed for high-traffic admission pages.
Business Impact: Enquiries are now more consistent, user trust improved, and the admissions team receives better-quality leads with less drop-off.
